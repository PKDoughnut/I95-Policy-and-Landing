I95: Safety Run

You're late for your shift, and the Safety Officer is on your tail.

Run the I-95, the crew corridor that goes the length of a cruise ship. Weave through trolleys and crew, report the rules being broken around you, and don't get caught.

How it plays

One corridor. Everyone's in it.

01

Swipe to weave

Step left and right between waiters, deck crew and loaded trolleys. Bump into someone and you lose pace and 100 points. Hit a trolley and it's off to Medical.

02

Stay ahead

The Safety Officer walks at the pace you should be keeping. Slow down and he closes in. When he raises his hand, you are nearly out of time.

03

Tap to report

The corridor is full of people breaking the rules. Spot one, tap it, and you score. Get it wrong and it costs you three times as much.
