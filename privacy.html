Privacy Policy for I95: Safety Run

Last updated: 10 October 2026

I95: Safety Run ("the game") is a mobile game published under the package name com.pkartintel.i95. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There are no advertisements.
  • There are no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

Information saved on your device

The game saves three things in its own storage on your device so that they are still there the next time you play:

  • your best score,
  • your achievements and the progress towards them,
  • whether you have turned the sound on or off.

This information never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

If you have device backup turned on, your device's operating system may include this saved data in its own backups (for example, to your Google account). That is done by your device under your backup settings, not by the game, and we have no access to it.

Permissions

The game requests the internet permission because the framework it is built with requires it. The game itself does not use the internet connection.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.
